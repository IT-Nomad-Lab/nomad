**Navigating AI Budgets as a CTO: What to Ask Before Approving**

As a Chief Technology Officer, I've been asked to greenlight numerous AI projects, each promising to revolutionize our business. But before I approve a single dollar, I ask myself a series of tough questions. Here's what I've learned are the essential queries to ask before allocating an AI budget:

1. **What specific problem are we trying to solve?** I want to understand the exact pain point we're addressing and how AI can provide a tangible solution. This ensures we're not chasing a trendy technology without a clear business case.

2. **What's the current state of our data?** AI is only as good as the data it's fed. I need to know if our data is clean, accurate, and comprehensive. If not, we risk reinforcing biases or getting subpar results.

3. **What's the expected ROI?** I'm not looking for unsubstantiated claims. I want to see a clear financial justification for the investment, including projections on revenue growth, cost savings, and potential risks.

4. **How will we measure success?** KPIs are crucial to evaluate the effectiveness of our AI solution. I need to know what metrics we'll use to assess its performance and whether it's meeting our expectations.

5. **What's the plan for ongoing maintenance and updates?** AI is not a one-and-done solution. I need to know who will be responsible for ensuring the system stays relevant and effective over time.

By asking these questions, I can make informed decisions about where to allocate our AI budget. It's not just about throwing money at a new technology; it's about driving meaningful change and growth for our business. What questions do you ask before approving an AI budget?
