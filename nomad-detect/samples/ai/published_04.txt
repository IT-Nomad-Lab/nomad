97% of companies are building AI agents. 12% know what those agents are actually doing. That number is not a prediction. It is the current state, published this week.

Companies rush to deploy before they understand the tool. That pattern is not new. We saw it with SaaS. We saw it with cloud. Every time a technology moves faster than governance, the same thing happens: things run in production that nobody owns, nobody audited, and nobody can fully explain.

Shadow IT gave us data silos and compliance headaches. Rogue SaaS apps were annoying. They sat there, they held data and they didn't act.

Agents are acting. They query databases, trigger workflows, send communications, make decisions. But an uncontrolled agent is not a data silo. It is an autonomous process running in your production environment with no owner on record. And most importantly, eating your budget.

97% exploring. 12% in control. That gap is not an adoption story. It is a governance emergency that most organisations have not named yet.

You do not need to slow down AI adoption. You need to know what you have already deployed.
