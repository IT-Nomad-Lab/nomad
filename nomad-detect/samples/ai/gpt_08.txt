**The Case for Owning Your Data Platform: A Path to True Data Sovereignty**

As a seasoned data professional, I've had the privilege of working with various organizations on their data platform endeavors. In my experience, there's a growing trend towards buying pre-built data platforms, but I strongly believe that owning your data platform is the better approach. In this post, I'd like to share my insights on the benefits of building your own data platform.

When you buy a pre-built data platform, you're essentially leasing a solution that's designed to meet someone else's standards. You're beholden to the vendor's roadmap, support, and pricing. On the other hand, owning your data platform allows you to design and build a solution that's tailored to your unique needs, without being tied to a vendor's constraints.

By owning your data platform, you gain complete control over your data architecture, allowing you to make strategic decisions about how your data is processed, stored, and analyzed. This not only reduces dependence on third-party vendors but also enables you to integrate your data platform with other internal systems and applications.

Moreover, owning your data platform empowers your organization to innovate and stay ahead of the curve. With a customized solution, you can experiment with new technologies, architectures, and methodologies, without being held back by vendor limitations. It's a path to true data sovereignty, where you have the freedom to make decisions about your data, unencumbered by external dependencies.

As data becomes increasingly critical to business success, I firmly believe that owning your data platform is the only way to ensure true data sovereignty.
