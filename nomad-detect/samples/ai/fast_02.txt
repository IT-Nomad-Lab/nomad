**Lessons from Rebuilding a Data Team**

As we've navigated our company's recent reorganization, I've been reminded of the importance of adaptability, clear communication, and a growth mindset. As the leader of our data team, I've seen firsthand the challenges that come with restructuring and the opportunities that arise from them.

One of the biggest takeaways for me has been the need to be intentional about change management. When the company reorganized, we had to rapidly adjust to new reporting lines, priorities, and stakeholders. But instead of resisting the changes, we took the opportunity to reassess our own processes and structure. We streamlined our data governance, updated our toolset, and clarified our roles and responsibilities. The result? We're now more agile, efficient, and effective than ever.

Another key lesson has been the value of clear communication. During periods of uncertainty, it's easy to let rumors and speculation spread. But by staying transparent and open with our stakeholders – including our leadership team, other departments, and customers – we've been able to build trust and confidence in our data team. This has not only helped us weather the transition but also enabled us to emerge stronger and more resilient.

Lastly, I've been reminded of the importance of a growth mindset. When our company reorganized, I could have focused on protecting our team's interests or preserving our existing way of working. But instead, I chose to see it as an opportunity for growth, learning, and innovation. By embracing the unknown and staying curious, we've been able to discover new ways of working, new tools, and new skills that will serve us well in the long term.

I'd love to hear from others who have navigated similar challenges. What lessons have you learned from restructuring or reorganizing your team?
