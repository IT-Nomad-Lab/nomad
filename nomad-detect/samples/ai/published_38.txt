I have sat in this meeting too many times and I'm sure that I'm not the only one:

  • Boss: "We need AI. Now."
  • Employee: "We can start with a few workflows."
  • Boss: "There is no time. The investors want to hear it on the call."
  • Employee: "Then we are not reviewing the data first."
  • Boss: "So have the AI check the AI."
  ... Three Doritos later ...
  • Boss: "The numbers are wrong."
  • Employee: "I know. We never reviewed the data."
  • Boss: "The bill is too high. You get half the budget."
  • Employee: "Fine."
  • Boss: "And you cover the work of three people."
  • Employee: "Because you let two of them go last week."
  • Boss: "Let us schedule a meeting about it."

Different words at every company. Same order every time.

We have seen this shape before. Crypto had it. NFTs had it. Commit first, find the use case later, because someone needs to say the word out loud to the board. The technology was never the problem. AI is useful. So was blockchain, in the handful of places it actually fit.

The comparison breaks at the end. When crypto fell over, the people who bought in lost their own money. This time the people who lost something were never in the room when the decision got made.

The bubble was never in the technology. It was in the mandate. And the bill went to people who never got to sign it.
