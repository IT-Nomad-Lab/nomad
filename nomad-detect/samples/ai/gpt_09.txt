**Before Approving that AI Budget: 5 Questions to Ask as a CTO**

As a CTO, I've seen firsthand the excitement around AI and its potential to drive innovation and growth. However, before approving a budget for AI initiatives, I always ask myself some tough questions to ensure we're making the right investment.

As we consider allocating resources to AI, I reflect on the following:

1. **What problem are we trying to solve?** Is AI the right tool for the job, or are there more cost-effective solutions? Have we explored other technologies or approaches that could achieve the same outcome?
2. **What are our key performance indicators (KPIs)?** How will we measure the success of our AI initiatives, and what metrics will we use to evaluate their impact on the business?
3. **What is the current state of our data?** Are we prepared to provide high-quality, well-structured data to support AI models, or do we need to invest in data preparation and enrichment?
4. **What are the long-term implications of AI on our organization?** Will AI replace existing roles, or create new ones? Have we considered the potential impact on our workforce and organizational structure?
5. **What is the timeline for ROI?** When can we expect to see tangible returns on our AI investment, and what's the plan for ongoing maintenance and updates?

By asking these questions, I ensure that our AI budget is aligned with business goals, and that we're making informed decisions about where to allocate resources. As a CTO, it's my responsibility to balance the excitement of new technology with the need for measured, data-driven decision-making.
